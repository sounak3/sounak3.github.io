Guides for the tools

These notes explain what each application does, how to get useful results from it, and where to find its source code. They are written for people deciding whether a tool fits their everyday workflow.

Using PlanetHour for a local day

PlanetHour calculates a day’s planetary-hour sequence from the local sunrise and sunset. Choose your country, city, coordinates, time zone and date in Settings, then open the expanded view to inspect the full table.

The daytime and nighttime periods are divided separately, so an hour is not assumed to be a fixed 60-minute clock hour. Use the displayed times as a planning reference rather than as a scientific or medical recommendation.

Converting Indian weight units

IndianGold is designed for conversions that involve traditional Indian units such as tola, bhori and ratti alongside standard units. Enter a value once and review the converted values together, instead of repeatedly selecting a different output unit.

Rates and unit conventions can differ by market and region. Treat the conversion as a calculation aid and confirm any transaction with the jeweller or authority responsible for the quoted rate.

Building a simple focus routine

DeskStop combines a desktop clock, multiple time zones, uptime information, alarms and a configurable Pomodoro timer. A practical starting routine is one focused interval, a short break, and a longer break after several intervals.

Use the settings to choose a clock style, sound and background that remain readable while you work. The application is open source, and the repository includes the licence and release files for each platform.
