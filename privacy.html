Privacy Policy

How sounaks.com handles your data. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Hosting and logs. This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses and request details for security and operations; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site itself stores no data about you.

 

Advertising. This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

 

Facebook. The IndianGold, PlanetHour and DeskStop pages include Facebook Like and Comments. These load Facebook's software, which may set cookies and receive information about your visit, and comments you post are handled by Facebook under the Meta Privacy Policy.

 

Donations. The "Support Now" buttons take you to PayPal. Payments and the details you enter there are handled by PayPal, not by this site.

 

Contact. If you e-mail the address on the Contact Us page, your message and e-mail address are used only to reply to you.

 

Downloads. Software downloads are plain files; downloading them does not collect any personal information beyond the hosting logs above.
